What we do

We build the shell. Everything else depends on it.

We are a South Florida shell contractor. General contractors, developers, and custom builders bring us in to put the structure in the ground and take it up: footings and slabs, CMU and reinforced masonry, cast-in-place columns and tie beams, post-tensioned decks, and the formwork, shoring, and steel placing that hold it all together. We hand off a shell that's plumb, square, and ready for dry-in.

Everything we build is engineered for the High Velocity Hurricane Zone: Miami-Dade approved assemblies, documented rebar placement, and inspections passed the first time. A shell that slips a week pushes every trade behind it a week.

We run self-performed crews and own our forms and shoring, and we give straight answers on schedule and price.
