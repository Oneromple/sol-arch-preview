Sol-Arch About

Shell is all we do.

We're a shell contractor based in Miami, building the structural core of commercial, multifamily, and custom residential projects across South Florida.

General contractors and developers hire us for the part of the job with the least margin for error. If the shell is late or out of tolerance, every trade after us pays for it. So we staff it with our own crews, run our own formwork and shoring, and take responsibility for the whole package from the first footing to the last deck pour.

What we do

  1. 01

    Concrete & Masonry Shell

    Footings, foundations, and slabs on grade. CMU and reinforced masonry walls. Cast-in-place columns, beams, tie beams, and stairs. Grouting, cells, and bond beams placed and documented to the engineer's drawings, not to whatever fits the schedule that week.

  2. 02

    Post-Tension & Vertical Concrete

    Post-tensioned slabs and elevated decks for mid-rise and high-rise work. Vertical concrete, core walls, and floor cycles run on a repeating schedule your other trades can actually plan around. We coordinate stressing, tendon layout, and inspection sequencing so the deck cycle holds.

  3. 03

    Rebar, Formwork & Shoring

    We place our own steel and own our forming and shoring systems, which is why our pours land on schedule. Reshoring plans, deck layouts, and load calculations are handled in-house and coordinated with your structural engineer before the first form goes up.

  4. 04

    Turnkey to Dry-In

    When you want a single point of accountability through the roof deck, we take the shell all the way: structure, roof deck, window and door bucks, and stucco prep, delivered ready for dry-in. One contract, one schedule, one number to call.

  5. 05

    Built for the HVHZ

    Every project we touch sits in Florida's High Velocity Hurricane Zone. That shapes how we build: Miami-Dade NOA-approved assemblies, wind-load-compliant connections, documented rebar and grout placement, and threshold inspection coordination handled as part of the job rather than a scramble at the end. We build shells that pass inspection the first time and stand up to a Category 5 design event.
